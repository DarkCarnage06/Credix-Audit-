# Economics

## What a Converted Lead is Worth to Credex

Credex sells discounted AI infrastructure credits at ~20-40% below retail. Average startup buying credits spends $500-2000/month on AI tools. At 30% discount on $1000/month spend, Credex margin is approximately $100-200/month per customer. Assuming 12-month retention, LTV per converted customer = **$1,200 - $2,400**.

Conservative estimate: **$1,500 LTV per converted customer**.

If week 1 gets 10 consultations booked, CAC = $50 per consultation.

## Conversion Funnel

- Audit completed → Email captured: **25-35%**
- Email captured → Consultation booked: **10-15%** (only high savings cases shown the CTA)
- Consultation booked → Credit purchase: **30-40%**
- Overall: Audit completed → Purchase: **~1-2%**

At 1000 audits/month → 10-20 purchases/month → $15,000-$30,000 MRR from the tool alone.

## What Would Have to Be True for $1M ARR in 18 Months

$1M ARR = ~$83,000 MRR = ~55 active customers at $1,500 average monthly spend through Credex.

**Month 1-3:** 500 audits/month, 1% conversion = 5 customers/month
**Month 4-9:** SEO kicks in, 2000 audits/month, 1.5% conversion = 30 customers/month
**Month 10-18:** Word of mouth + referrals, 5000 audits/month, 2% conversion = 100 customers/month

Assumptions that must hold:
- Average customer spends $1,500/month through Credex
- Churn stays below 5%/month
- Audit completion rate stays above 30%
- High-savings rate (>$500/mo) stays above 15% of audits

This is achievable if Credex focuses distribution on engineering managers at funded startups (higher AI spend) rather than solo developers.

## Show the Math

Monthly target at month 18: $83,000 MRR

New customers needed per month (at 5% churn): ~3-4 net new
Audits needed at 2% conversion: ~200 audits/month
This is very achievable — a single viral HN post drives this in one day.
